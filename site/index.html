Risk questions, answered with their uncertainty

Keanu is a probabilistic programming library in Rust. These demos run it in your browser: each one fits a Bayesian model to data as you watch, then answers a practical question with a range rather than a single guess. Change the inputs and the answer updates.

  • How much could this position lose tomorrow?

    Volatility models of daily returns, value at risk and expected shortfall. Paste your own prices.

    Fits in under a second

  • How many of our loans will default, and what will it cost?

    Default rates learned from a loan history, then a loan book priced under a downturn you choose.

    Fits in a few seconds
